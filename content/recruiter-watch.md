# Recruiter Watch

The fourth news lane: what's happening at companies that hire MBA graduates.

**Idea:** students shouldn't only prepare for "dream" recruiters. A student
interviewing at a mid-size regional company deserves the same quality of
preparation. So we offer two things:
1. **A starter list** of well-known MBA recruiters by sector, which the news lane tracks daily.
2. **A company profile template** any student can use for *any* company, even
   one that's rarely in the news, by comparing it with its sector leader.

Status: **DRAFT**, for your review. Company names only, no claims yet.
Every fact we later add to a profile must be sourced and from 2024 onwards.

## Part 2 · Company profile template (works for any company)

Fill this in for each company. For a small company with little news, use the
**"compare with the leader"** row: interviewers love candidates who know where
their company stands against the biggest player.

| # | Section | Question it answers | Example (HUL, illustrative only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What they do** | One line: what does the company sell, and to whom? | Everyday home & personal care products to Indian households |
| 2 | **How they make money** | Main revenue sources | Soaps, detergents, food & beverages, beauty |
| 3 | **Who they compete with** | Top 2–3 rivals | ITC, P&G, Dabur |
| 4 | **Compare with the leader** | If not the leader: how are they different? Cheaper? Regional? Niche? | (HUL *is* the leader) |
| 5 | **Recent news (2024+)** | 2–3 sourced stories from the last 12 months | Filled in from the news lane |
| 6 | **Concepts that connect** | Which MBA concepts explain their situation? | Premiumisation → **Income Elasticity** (Card 4) |
| 7 | **Likely interview questions** | Questions they could ask, based on the above | "Why is premiumisation good for HUL's margins?" |
| 8 | **Smart question to ask them** | One thoughtful question the student can ask the interviewer | "How is rural premiumisation changing your distribution strategy?" |

### Why this matters for less-known companies
A student going to, say, a regional paint company can fill in rows 1–4 from the
company website, then borrow rows 6–7 from the sector leader (Asian Paints or
Berger). Same sector, same concepts, same kind of questions, so **the
preparation quality is the same, whatever the company's reputation.**
